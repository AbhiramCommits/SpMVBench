Generated by `tools/report.py` from the committed artifacts in `results/`.

**Baselines:** CPU = multithreaded OpenMP `csr_scalar_omp` (single-thread `csr_scalar_1t` shown only as context); GPU = `cusparse_spmv` / `cusparse_spmm`. Baseline rows are not compared against themselves.

**GPU:** Tesla T4 (sm_7.5). Assumed theoretical peak bandwidth: **320 GB/s** (vendor spec assumed from the device name). Achieved bandwidth uses the no-reuse traffic model.

GPU timings are medians over 50 CUDA-event iterations after warmup; CPU timings are medians over 10 steady-clock iterations. Kernel time excludes host-device transfers; `transfer` is the separate H2D+D2H time.

Bandwidth uses the no-reuse traffic model. Values above 100% of peak mean that model overcounts DRAM traffic because the input vector is partly served from cache; `bw_lo_gbs` in `bench.csv` is the compulsory-traffic lower bound.
